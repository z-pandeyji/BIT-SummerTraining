TITLE: Prompt Engineering

Definition

Prompt Engineering is the process of designing clear and effective instructions that guide Large Language Models (LLMs) to produce accurate, relevant, and useful responses. Well-designed prompts improve answer quality and reduce ambiguity.

How It Works

A prompt provides context, instructions, examples, and constraints to the language model. Techniques such as zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot prompting help models perform tasks more reliably.

Applications

Chatbots, coding assistants, document summarization, content generation, question answering, customer support, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Advantages

- Improves response quality.
- Requires no model retraining.
- Flexible and easy to experiment with.

Limitations

- Sensitive to wording.
- Different prompts may produce different outputs.
- Cannot compensate for missing knowledge.

Summary

Prompt Engineering helps users obtain more accurate and grounded responses by carefully structuring instructions given to language models.
