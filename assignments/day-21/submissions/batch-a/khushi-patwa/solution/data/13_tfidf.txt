TITLE: TF-IDF

Definition

TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) is a statistical technique used to measure the importance of a word within a document relative to a collection of documents. It is commonly used in document retrieval and text mining.

How It Works

TF measures how frequently a word appears in a document, while IDF reduces the importance of words that appear in many documents. Multiplying these values produces a score that highlights informative words.

Applications

Search engines, document ranking, text classification, document clustering, recommendation systems, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Advantages

- Simple and efficient.
- Fast computation.
- Effective for keyword matching.

Limitations

- Cannot understand semantic relationships.
- Sensitive to vocabulary differences.
- Performs poorly with synonyms.

Summary

TF-IDF provides a simple yet powerful way to rank documents using keyword importance and serves as a strong baseline retrieval method.
